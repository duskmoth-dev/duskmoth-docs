# Getting started

Open https://duskmoth.xyz/app. Connect Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack through its browser extension.
Never enter a seed phrase or private key into chat.
Refresh restores only a connection that the extension still authorizes. Disconnect is available in the header and Wallet pane.
Explicit Disconnect remains disconnected after refresh; disconnecting does not cancel a transaction already submitted.

Useful AI instructions:

```text
Show my balance
Check my privacy score
```

A score requires a message signature proving ownership, not a funds transfer. It is a heuristic, not a safety certificate.
Keep SOL for network fees even when transferring USDC.
